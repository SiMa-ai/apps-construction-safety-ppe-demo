# SiMa Site Watch

Construction safety monitoring demo using camera video, configured safety zones, PPE detection, and recorded incident evidence.

## Tasks

Watch available camera views, inspect danger-zone and missing-PPE findings, open their annotated evidence, and configure camera routing and safety zones. Runtime camera names and availability come from the connected sources.

## Product truth

Findings are grouped review records, not counts of unique people or real-world accidents. Track IDs belong to an individual camera session; they do not establish cross-camera identity. Detections support human review and do not establish verified safety compliance. Review collection begins when monitoring or a new review starts, rather than being synchronized to a clip's first frame. Prior reviews and original audit records are preserved.

## Interface direction

The user supplied dark smart-camera references: rounded charcoal surfaces and lime selected navigation from the first reference, with a video-first camera wall and compact adjacent alert feed from the other references. Keep visible wording short. Put diagnostics, review explanations, filters, and secondary actions behind disclosures. Keep camera settings accessible. Do not add a pause control or a redundant review link to the monitor.

## Content boundaries

Use connected camera data and actual evidence. Do not invent site names, incident counts, benchmarks, identity guarantees, or compliance claims. Distinguish connecting, unavailable, and empty states from healthy monitoring. Keep implementation terminology in diagnostic or configuration contexts where it supports a task.
